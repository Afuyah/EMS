Lateness & absenteeism rules

Two independent ladders, both scopeable to a single department or left global. A department rule always overrides the global rule of the same kind.

Before tuning these: what counts as "late" is decided by the shift & lateness policy — arrival time, grace period and the very-late threshold. These ladders only act on the resulting counts.

Attendance health ladder

Sets the employee badge colour. Applied nightly by color_recompute at 02:30. A rule matches when both thresholds are reached.

Disciplinary ladder

Raises warnings and suspensions. Applied nightly by disciplinary_evaluator at 03:00. A rule matches when its single counter reaches the threshold.

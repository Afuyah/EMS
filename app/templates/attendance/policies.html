{% endcall %} {% call card('How a punch is judged') %}
  1. The employee's department policy is used, or the global default if it has none.
  2. A punch at or before shift start + grace is on time.
  3. A punch after that is late, and becomes very late once it passes the very-late threshold.
  4. Late minutes feed the colour ladder and the disciplinary thresholds, so a very late arrival counts towards those automatically.
  5. If a cut-off is set and no punch has arrived by then, the day is marked absent without waiting for the nightly job.
